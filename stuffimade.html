stories from spring

my fantasy worldbuilding project. the "standard" fantasy setting is often stuck in the medieval era, but the medieval era is honestly kinda boring (in my opinon) compared to some other historical periods, so i wanted to make a story focused on a magical world changing throughout multiple time periods. the current developed lore of stories from spring mostly focuses on the region of calanna throughout the modern era, and the region of east calam in the late modern era.
